FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Betroverse offer?

We specialize in full-scale video production and digital marketing. This includes corporate films, brand promotional videos, product shoot videography, high-engaging social media content (Reels/Shorts), event videography, and client testimonial filming.

How long does a video production project take?

Typically, a standard project takes 1 to 3 weeks. This timeframe covers all phases: pre-production scripting and storyboarding, actual on-site filming, and post-production video editing with music licensing and color grading.

Can we customize our packages and plans?

Yes, absolutely! Every brand has unique goals and constraints. We work closely with our clients to customize production volumes, marketing channels, and strategy elements to suit their budget and targets.

Do you handle social media distribution?

Yes, we do. In our Growth and Enterprise plans, we include full social media strategy, posting schedules, caption writing, and performance tracking across channels like Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok to ensure maximum reach.

How do we get started?

Getting started is easy! You can click the "Get Started" CTA button on the navigation bar, fill in our contact form below, or send us a message directly on WhatsApp. We will connect with you within 24 hours to schedule a kickoff call.
